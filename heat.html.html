3. Heat Treatment (IN 561/HT) Services

We provide Heat Treatment (HT) services for wooden pallets, crates, plywood pallets, boxes, and packaging materials according to ISPM-15 export standards.

Wood packaging materials are treated in a controlled heat chamber where the core temperature is maintained according to prescribed international standards to eliminate pests and larvae.
